# Playbook: UGC and talking-head (incl. fintech brand deals)

From a three-video paid UGC campaign for a crypto perpetuals exchange: single-take selfie clips, educational and personality-led, posted as Instagram Reels.

## Script (before filming)

- Each video teaches one idea a newcomer gets wrong (for example, funding rates, leverage and liquidation, earnings-night volatility). Quiz the creator on the concepts first; where they stumble, the audience will too.
- Use the community's inside jokes and slang in the hook, and check each one is current.
- Write the brand line for the creator to **say**, not just show: "<Brand> is where I trade this." Spoken CTAs land better than text.
- Every video ends with a CTA: "comment your pick", "save this", "link in bio".

## Edit

- Tighten pauses at silences of about 0.3-0.5 s, but keep the creator's natural delivery and ad-libs; personality is the point.
- Trim hesitation pauses in the hook hardest. Drop trailing filler ("and…") before the payoff.
- Overlays: short labels timed to word timestamps, placed in empty space above the head. 2-3 word uppercase captions under the face.
- Cutaways: short, full-frame, from the brand's own docs, app screenshots or official mockups. Crop to the part that matters (the order book, the fee table) so numbers are readable on a phone. Label mockups honestly ("The <Brand> app") if they show a different market than the one discussed.
- Light grade (a gentle contrast/saturation punch). -14 LUFS.
- Sound effects and meme freezes (record scratch, mic drop, a 0.8 s freeze with a label) make educational videos fun. Synthesize your own or leave the moment silent for an in-app library sound; a paid post can't use ripped audio.

## Accuracy and compliance (finance)

- When the creator misspeaks (says "account" for "position", "50%" for "50x"), keep the audio, fix the caption, and add a small on-screen correction. Tell the user each one.
- Copy every number and term from the brand's own docs (fees, rebates, caps, how to claim). Show conditions in full ("Up to $500 per account per cycle, redeposit + trade to claim").
- Persistent quiet "Not financial advice" line. #ad or the platform's paid-partnership label.
- Check with the brand contact before using their official mockups and docs screenshots.
- Referral codes may have unlock thresholds; use the direct app link until the code works.

## Posting checklist

1. Add in-app library sounds at the listed timestamps (quieter than the voice).
2. Turn on paid partnership or add #ad (ask the brand which).
3. Brand link in bio.
4. Caption copy from the script, matching the corrected on-screen text.
