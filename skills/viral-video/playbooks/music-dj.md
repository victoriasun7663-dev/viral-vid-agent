# Playbook: DJ sets and music performance

From two projects: drop-led reels cut from phone clips of a bass/dubstep set, and transition reels cut from a full recorded DJ set on YouTube.

## Two reel types

**Drop reels** (one clip, one drop)
- Open about one bar before the drop with a POV hook on screen ("POV: it's 2am and she finally drops it"). One bar, not four; long builds lose people.
- Snap from a medium crop to a tight crop on the drop downbeat, with a small punch-in (1.12 to 1.0) and a 2-frame white flash on the kick.
- Ride the drop and end on a bar line so it loops.
- Double drops: jump-cut through the break, and let the fake-out be the joke ("POV: you thought that was the drop").

**Transition reels** (one song into the next)
- The viewer must **hear song 1, then song 2**. A reel that only plays one track fails, even if the label says "transition".
- A long overlay isn't required. Opening on a clearly-song-1 section and cutting straight to song 2's arrival or drop is good enough. Start early enough that song 1 is unmistakable.
- Before promising a blend, measure it: fingerprint the audio around each switch and only call it a blend where two songs actually play at once. Quick cuts and "song drops into its own breakdown" aren't overlays; bench them instead of mislabeling them.
- Label the stages honestly on the top line: "song 1…", "song 2 coming in…", "both songs at once", "NEXT SONG >>".

## Measuring

- Measure tempo from the audio; the BPM in a set's title is often wrong (144 vs a claimed 150). All cut lengths are whole bars.
- Find drops from the bass-band onset (first frame more than about 12 dB over the pre-drop level) and cross-check on a waveform.
- A timestamp the user gives may be a drop inside the next song rather than the switch. Check and say so.
- When two beat measurements disagree, flag the timestamp for the user's ear rather than guessing.

## Lyrics and text

- Music reels with no text feel like dead air. Add lyric pops: 2-3 words, uppercase, punchy, through the whole reel.
- Identify the tracks first (tracklist, description, comments, web search). Use transcription for timing only.
- You can't copy lyrics from lyric sites. Ask the user to paste lines, or use transcription and mark every uncertain word as unverified.
- Color-code singers or songs (song 1 white, song 2 cyan) when two vocals overlap.
- Bleep profanity on screen (***) even if the audio stays.
- Extend reels to musical phrase boundaries (8 or 16 bars) so lyrics have room; 25-45 s works.

## Picture

- Reframe the tight crop per bar to keep the DJ in frame; they move around the decks.
- Deflicker strobe segments. Crop out other apps' watermarks.

## Audio

- -12 LUFS, -1 dBTP. One continuous audio extract per reel; separate seeks skip.
- Only use music the user owns or has cleared for the post.

## Posting checklist

1. Track names in the caption where known; "ID?" invites comments where not.
2. Confirm music rights for the platform (original mix vs. label release).
3. Pick a cover frame on the drop.
